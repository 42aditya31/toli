## Interpretations where the docs are silent (recorded, not new rules)

1. **Kitty balance sign.** The engine returns `kittyCash` (cash in the pot, shown as "₹3,000") and `kittyBalance = −kittyCash` (the virtual member K's balance). The invariant is **Σ trip balances + kittyBalance = 0**. That's what 07 §6 means by "members + the kitty line sum to 0" when the line is counted as K's balance.
2. **Hand-over in the settle view** (07 §6, D-022). Each holding period gets a `kitty_holding` line of −(net cash that came in during the period). Each hand-over adds `kitty_handover` lines: **+R** for the giver and **−R** for the receiver, where R is the cash at that moment. The totals are identical to "charge the current holder with the final remaining" (07 §6), and both holders' traces show the hand-over, as D-022 requires.
3. **Direct mode with a kitty** (07 §8.2): kitty lines pair with the **current** holder. Direct-mode nets then equal settle-view balances (invariant 11).
4. **Zero entries** are dropped from the share maps (a member with 0 isn't a participant).
5. **Top-up** uses the exact rational `spent × daysLeft ÷ daysElapsed − remaining`, rounded **up** to ₹50 per person (07 §6 names no rounding for `daily`).
6. **Removed members** (D-030, invariant 12): `checkRemovedMembers(before, after)` rejects any change to a removed member's trip balance. The "no new share or payment" rule is an op-validation check in `sync-push` (M5).
