# D-015 · Welcome screen direction
| Status | Draft | Date | 2026-09-24 |
|---|---|---|---|

## Problem
`Toli Welcome Variants` explores five directions:
- 1a type-led;
- **1b the screen is the ticket**;
- 1c receipt printing;
- 1d the group ring;
- 1e one number (60 → 3).

## Decision
Use **1b**, which is the one built into the clickable prototype:
- a lime top half ("ADMIT ALL", "One trip. One tab. Zero awkward.") with a dashed perforation and notches;
- below it the line "Unlimited expenses and every kind of split, free. Toli works out who pays whom.";
- **Get started** and **I have an invite link** buttons.

## Why
It's already the prototype's choice, and it matches the ticket brand.

## Trade-offs
1c and 1e are strong alternatives for onboarding marketing or store screenshots.
